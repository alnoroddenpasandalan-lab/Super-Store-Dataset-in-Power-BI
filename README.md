# 📊 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built in **Power BI** to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, geographic distribution, shipping trends, and transaction-level sales data using the **Superstore Sales Dataset**.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Intelligence workflow—from data preparation in **Power Query** to data modeling and analytical reporting using **DAX** and interactive Power BI dashboards.

The report is designed for business users and decision-makers to monitor sales performance, identify trends, evaluate customer behavior, and drill down into detailed transactions.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.
- Build an executive dashboard for KPI monitoring.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate regional sales performance.
- Monitor shipping patterns.
- Enable interactive filtering and drill-down analysis.

---

# 📈 Dashboard Pages

## 1. Executive Sales Overview

Provides a high-level summary of overall business performance.

### KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Sales per Order

### Visuals

- Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Sub-Category

### Business Questions Answered

- How is the business performing?
- Which category generates the highest revenue?
- Which region contributes the most sales?
- Which products are driving revenue?

## 2. Customer Analysis

Focuses on customer purchasing behavior and customer segmentation.

### KPIs

- Maximum Sales per Order
- Minimum Sales per Order
- Average Sales per Order

### Visuals

- Top 10 Customers
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Number of Customers by Segment

### Business Questions Answered

- Who are the highest-value customers?
- Which customer segment generates the most revenue?
- Which segment contains the largest customer base?

## 3. Geographic Analysis

Analyzes sales performance across different locations.
